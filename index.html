discommand

A small, vim-style terminal client for your chats, calendar and tasks.

discommand brings Discord, WhatsApp, Matrix, Google Calendar, Microsoft To Do and Twos into one keyboard-driven terminal app. It's written in Rust, redraws only when something changes and runs on Windows, Linux and macOS.

It's free and open source. Everything runs on your own computer.
