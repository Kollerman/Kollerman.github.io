Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2, 2026

discommand is an open-source terminal app that runs entirely on your own computer. There is no discommand server, and the app has no analytics or telemetry.

What data the app accesses

When you sign in to a service (Discord, WhatsApp, Matrix, Google Calendar, Microsoft To Do or Twos), discommand reads and changes your data on that service only to show it to you and to carry out what you ask, such as sending a message or adding a calendar event.

Where data is stored

Sign-in tokens and settings are stored locally on your computer. Your data is sent only directly between your computer and the service it belongs to. It is never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Google user data

discommand uses Google Calendar data only to display your calendars and events and to create, edit or delete events at your request. It is not used for advertising, not sold, and not shared with anyone. discommand's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Sign out in the app or delete its configuration folder to remove stored tokens. You can also revoke access at any time from your Google account's third-party access page.
